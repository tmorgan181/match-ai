Claude Chrome: 

**Emotional/personal relationship with AI**
- Have you ever felt uncomfortable after an interaction with an AI? (yes/sometimes/no)
- Do you think AI understands you better or worse than most people in your life?
- Have you ever felt deceived by an AI system?

**Values and tradeoffs** (these force people off the fence)
- If AI could eliminate a disease but put 10,000 researchers out of work, would that be a good trade?
- Should AI systems be allowed to refuse requests? (yes/no/depends)
- Who should be most responsible for AI harm — developers, platforms, or governments?

**Identity and labor**
- Has your work or creative practice changed because of AI?
- Do you think your job will look meaningfully different in 5 years because of AI?

**Trust and power**
- Which institution do you trust most to govern AI? (government / big tech / academia / open source community / none)
- Do you think the people building AI share your values?

**Wildcard that I think is underexplored**
- Do you think AI should have any rights or protections? Ever? (this would beautifully separate archetypes in unexpected ways)

The tradeoff questions and the "who do you trust" question would be especially powerful for distinguishing archetypes that currently might look similar on the surface — a Guardian who trusts government regulation versus one who trusts grassroots organizing are quite different people.

Who should be primarily responsible for governing AI — governments, tech companies, international bodies, open-source communities, or no one?
